Most side projects don't fail in the code.
They fail on launch day, with a post nobody reads.

That was me, several times.

So I built launch-kit: a free skill for AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor).

You type one command in your project. It reads what you built and writes:
→ a Hacker News post
→ Product Hunt copy
→ Reddit posts tailored to each community
→ an X thread and a LinkedIn post
→ a 14-day launch plan

What I learned writing it:
1. Each platform has its own culture. The same post everywhere fails everywhere.
2. Honesty sells. Admitting one limitation builds more trust than any adjective.
3. Specific beats clever. "Runs on your GPU" beats "AI-powered".
