# Product Hunt

**Name:** launch-kit
**Tagline** (48 chars): Your repo's whole launch, written in one command
**Description** (≤260): A free skill for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor. It reads your code and writes Show HN, Product Hunt, Reddit, X and LinkedIn posts, a README glow-up, a demo script and a 14-day launch plan. No invented claims.
**Topics:** Developer Tools, Marketing, Artificial Intelligence

**Maker comment**

Hey PH 👋

I've shipped side projects that nobody ever saw, because writing launch posts felt like a second job and I was bad at it.

launch-kit is a free, open-source skill for your AI coding agent. Type /launch-kit in any repo and it reads what you built, picks a hook, and writes every launch post, each in the style of its platform.

My favourite rule: it's not allowed to invent anything. No fake stats, no fake testimonials, and a banned-word list for hype.

What should it write next: a newsletter pitch, or a dev.to article?
