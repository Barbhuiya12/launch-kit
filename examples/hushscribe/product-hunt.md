# Product Hunt

**Name:** Hushscribe
**Tagline** (50 chars): Private AI transcripts & subtitles in your browser
**Description** (≤260): Drop audio or video and get a transcript plus SRT/VTT subtitles. Whisper AI runs on your own device, so nothing is uploaded. 90+ languages, click-to-seek transcript, works offline after the first load. Free up to 10 min; one-time Pro.
**Topics:** Productivity, Artificial Intelligence, Privacy

**Maker comment**

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I kept needing transcripts of interviews and screen recordings, and every option meant uploading private audio to someone's server and paying monthly.

So I built Hushscribe. The Whisper speech model downloads once into your browser and runs on your GPU (or CPU). Your file never leaves your device. You can literally turn off Wi-Fi after it loads.

You get a live transcript you can click to jump around the video, plus SRT/VTT exports for YouTube, Premiere, CapCut or DaVinci.

Free for files up to 10 minutes. Pro is a one-time payment, not a subscription.

Next up: speaker labels. What would you use it for?
